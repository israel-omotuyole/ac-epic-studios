About

The Story Behind The Lens

A&C Epic Studios

We are a dedicated video production and photography company focused on immortalizing celebrations. Whether it is an emotional wedding vow, a vibrant milestone birthday, or a corporate milestone, we believe that authentic visual storytelling comes from genuine observation, not forced directing.

Operating out of Washington State, we bring state-of-the-art 4K cinema optics, professional directional audio, and an in-house editing suite to make sure every project looks magazine-worthy and timeless.
